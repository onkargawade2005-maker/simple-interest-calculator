# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

The Simple Interest Calculator calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:

* **Principal:** The initial amount of money.
* **Rate:** The annual interest rate in percentage.
* **Time:** The time period in years.

## Example

* Principal: ₹10,000
* Rate: 5%
* Time: 2 years

Calculation:

Simple Interest = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = ₹1,000

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest = ₹11,000

## Features

* Calculates simple interest.
* Calculates the total amount.
* Easy to understand and use.
